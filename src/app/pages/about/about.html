DRM-Free Forever

When you buy a book here, you own it — full stop. No proprietary readers, no licence expiry, no account required to open your own files. Download it once and read it on any device, share it with a friend, or archive it for decades. Your books travel with you, not with a platform.

Priced to Be Read

Expensive books get left on wish lists. We keep prices deliberately low because we believe the best return on a book is actually reading it. No subscriptions, no bundles you didn't ask for — just honest, one-time pricing that makes saying “yes” to a new read an easy decision.
